Who’s who in an HUF Demat Account

Hindu, Jain, Sikh and Buddhist families can form an HUF. Once it has a PAN and a bank account, it can open a demat and trading account like any investor.

  • Karta. The senior member who opens, operates and signs for the account.
  • Coparceners. Members by birth, sons and daughters alike, who share in the HUF’s assets.
  • Members. Others in the family, such as the Karta’s spouse, who are part of the HUF.
